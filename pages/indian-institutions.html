Sanskrit Higher Education in India

India hosts a wide ecosystem of Sanskrit institutions ranging from dedicated Sanskrit universities to departments embedded within multidisciplinary institutions. These institutions contribute to textual preservation, pedagogy, manuscriptology, Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS), computational linguistics, Vedic studies, and interdisciplinary Sanskrit research.

This section combines institution directories, academic program details, geographic distribution, specialization areas, and research infrastructure into a unified digital reference system.
